# Phygital Atlas

Phygital Atlas is an immersive journey for students on one of my modules, called Emerging Markets.

**Play it here:** https://irinagokhai-commits.github.io/phygital-atlas

## What it is

Phygital Atlas is a short interactive journey through seven places: a shore, a trading floor, a mountain glacier, a river, a city, a cabin and a ship at sea. Each place holds one small exercise that stands on its own. Along the way, students gather the evidence they need for the web page they make for the module.

"Phygital" means physical, digital and AI, blended together. It is my picture of how work will feel in the near future, and the game lets students step into that world for a while.

## How to play

Open the link in any browser. There is nothing to install and no sign-in. It is best played alone, with headphones, at your own pace.

## About me

I'm Irina Gokh, an educator who loves to experiment, especially with AI, and to share what I learn so that others can try it too.

My students' assignment is holistic: it asks them to use very different skills and to connect the dots between the things we study during the module. I believe that to understand how to do something, you need to start doing it. Phygital Atlas is my way of helping students start and connect those dots.

This is the very first version of the game. It follows the assignment students submit and brings in some of the activities I designed for the module.
